Steward privacy policy

Steward is a personal tasks, notes and calendar app run by Charles Smith for his own use. This page explains what the app does with Google account data.

What Steward accesses

When you connect a Google account, Steward asks only for per-file Google Drive access: it can see and change the files it creates itself (its data files and attachments in a folder named "Steward" in your Drive) and nothing else in your Drive. Google Calendar, and files Claude adds to the Steward folder for you, are reached through the "Steward helper", a Google Apps Script that runs in your own Google account; it shows, creates, changes and deletes the calendar events you choose to work with, and keeps a calendar named "Steward Reminders" for task reminders.

Where your data goes

Your tasks, notes, attachments and calendar information are stored only in your own browser on your own devices and in your own Google account. Steward has no server of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other party, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google user data

Steward's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you see in the app.

Removing access and data

You can disconnect at any time in Steward's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your data, delete the "Steward" folder from your Google Drive and clear the site's data in your browser.
